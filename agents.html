// every plan · Front Office Core, $497/mo

What every client gets.

voice/

AI voice receptionist

Inbound only, on its own local number. You forward missed and after-hours calls to it. It says it’s an AI and that the call is recorded, answers from your FAQs, books into your Google or Outlook calendar, takes messages and transfers urgent calls to your cell. 300 minutes a month.

learn more →
chat/

AI website chat

Answers visitors on your website. For the first 14 days it only suggests replies; it answers on its own once you sign off.

booking/

Booking and reminders

Booking confirmations, 24-hour and 1-hour reminders, and a no-show rebook email.

text/

Missed-call text-back

Texts back callers you missed. It switches on once the carriers approve your business’s texting registration (A2P 10DLC); those carrier fees and your text usage are billed at cost.

reviews/

Review requests

Asks customers for a review after completed appointments.

consent/

Consent and opt-outs

Separate, unchecked text and AI-call consent boxes on your forms. One opt-out stops every channel. Automated texts only between 8 AM and 8 PM in the contact’s time zone.

dashboard/

Live dashboard

Calls answered, after-hours calls caught, bookings, no-shows, reviews and speed-to-lead.

reporting/

Weekly results email

A short email every week built from your real numbers, plus a 30-minute monthly check-in and one change a month (FAQs, hours or script).

account/

Your own Efficio account

A HighLevel sub-account branded Efficio: CRM, unified inbox, pipeline, calendar, reviews and the mobile app. Your data lives there and belongs to you.

Managed adds.

minutes/

1,000 voice minutes

Instead of 300 a month on Core. Minutes over the allowance are $0.25 each.

tuning/

2 hours of tuning a month

Brady reviews calls and tunes your FAQs, script and workflows.

automation/

One new automation a month

From a set menu: a web-form speed-to-lead reply, estimate or quote follow-up, email reactivation of past customers who agreed to hear from you, or an intake form.

ask/

“Ask Efficio”

Ask plain-English questions about your own live data, like “which leads this week have no follow-up?”. It uses a read-only connection and never sends anything.

revenue/

Custom revenue dashboard

From about day 45: your calls, bookings and pipeline joined with Stripe or QuickBooks revenue, read-only.

review/

60-minute monthly review

A longer monthly call to go through the numbers and decide what to change next.

// what we don’t do

Not part of any plan.

  • Outbound AI calls or texts to anyone without documented consent, and no cold outreach, robocalls or purchased-list dialing.
  • Anything involving patient health information, so we don’t take on dental or medical practices.
  • Legal, tax, medical or financial advice from the AI. For law firms, the receptionist does scheduling and message-taking only.
  • Bookkeeping, and self-serve sign-up. Every account is set up by hand after a call.
