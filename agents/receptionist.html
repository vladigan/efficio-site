// what it handles

First contact, handled honestly.

It answers inbound calls only. It never cold-calls anyone.

Answers with a disclosure

Every call opens by saying it’s an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. It answers from the FAQs, hours and prices you approve.

Books the appointment

Checks your Google or Outlook calendar and books the caller into an open slot. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically.

Transfers what’s urgent

Takes a message for anything it can’t handle and transfers urgent calls to your cell, following the rules you set.

// how it works

Five steps. You can review the script.

1

A call goes unanswered

You forward missed and after-hours calls from your carrier to the receptionist’s number.

2

It answers

With an AI and call-recording disclosure, in the tone and wording you approved.

3

It captures the details

Name, number, what they need and how urgent it is, saved to your Efficio account.

4

It books or transfers

Books into your calendar, takes a message, or transfers an urgent call to you.

5

It shows up on your dashboard

Every call, booking and message, plus a weekly results email.

// the details

What to know before you buy.

  • Minutes: 1,000 a month on every plan. Extra minutes are $0.25 each.
  • Texting: missed-call text-back starts once the carriers approve your business’s texting registration (A2P 10DLC). The voice receptionist doesn’t need that approval.
  • Your number stays yours. The receptionist uses a new local number that you forward calls to.
  • Law firms: scheduling and message-taking only, no legal advice. We don’t set it up for practices that handle patient health information.
