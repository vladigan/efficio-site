After you sign — what we ask for

Your part: about an hour, total.

A short intake form, a kickoff call, turning on call forwarding and connecting your calendar. Everything below is exactly what we ask for and why.

1

The intake form

~10 min

Sent right after you sign, so you can fill it in on your own time. We use it to write the receptionist’s script and FAQs:

  • Hours and service areaWhen you’re open, and where you work.
  • Services and the prices the AI may quoteOnly what you approve. Anything else becomes “the owner will follow up.”
  • Common questions and your answersThe things callers ask every day.
  • Where urgent calls should goThe number the receptionist transfers urgent calls to.
  • Your calendar and websiteGoogle or Outlook calendar, and the site where chat will go.
  • Legal business name and EINThe carriers require these for your texting (A2P 10DLC) registration.
2

Kickoff call and access

~40 min
  • 30-minute kickoff callWe go through your intake together. We create your Efficio account (a HighLevel sub-account) and send you a login.
  • Connect your calendarGoogle or Outlook, through the calendar’s own sign-in screen. We never see your password.
  • Turn on call forwardingForward unanswered and after-hours calls to the receptionist’s new local number. We send instructions for your carrier.
  • 20-minute go-live callYou place a test call and we walk through your dashboard.

  • Your account, your data.Contacts, calls and conversations live in your own Efficio account on HighLevel, separate from other clients, and we don’t use them to train AI models.
  • A short list of providers.To run the service, data passes through HighLevel (LeadConnector), Anthropic, Cloudflare, Stripe, Google Workspace and Brevo. Details in our privacy policy.
  • Read-only where it matters.On the Founding CTO and Full Custom CTO plans, “Ask Efficio” and the custom dashboard use read-only connections that can’t send anything, and you can revoke them.
  • When you leave.You get a CSV export of your contacts and a 30-day wind-down before the account is closed.
