AI for plumbing companies: AI agents for the office work, one dashboard for every app

Efficio · May 29, 2026 · Updated Oct 22, 2026 · ~6 min read

AI for a plumbing company means AI agents doing the office jobs that pile up while you and your crew are under sinks: sorting and routing new jobs, following up on quotes, chasing unpaid invoices, booking and reminding, and sending you the week’s numbers. With Efficio, all of it runs across the apps you already use, sits on one live CEO dashboard, and is built and run for you by a fractional AI CTO.

The real bottleneck in a plumbing company is the office

Owner-led plumbing shops rarely lose work because the plumbing was bad. They lose it in the gaps: the repipe quote nobody followed up on, the invoice that sat for six weeks, the water heater customer who was never reminded about a flush, the job request that came in through the website form while everyone was on a call.

Most of that work is spread across apps that don’t talk to each other. Your field software has the jobs, QuickBooks has the money, Google has the calendar and email, and your phone has the texts. Pulling a straight answer out of all that means logging into each one, which is why it usually happens late at night or not at all.

For example, a typical five-truck shop might find out on a Friday that three quotes from Monday never got a second touch and two invoices are past due. Not because anyone dropped the ball, but because none of it was anyone’s job.

What AI agents do in a plumbing company, job by job

Each agent has one named job, works inside your own Efficio account, and sticks to the wording, prices and rules you approve.

Job intake and triage. A custom intake agent reads each new request, whether it came from a form, chat or a forwarded call, and sorts it: active leak or no water, a drain that can wait until Thursday, a remodel or repipe quote, a commercial bid. It collects the address and details and routes urgent work to whoever is on call, with everything they need to roll.

Quote follow-up. Bigger tickets like water heater swaps, sewer line work and repipes are where homeowners compare bids. The follow-up agent checks in a day after the quote and again a few days later, in your words, and stops when the customer replies or books.

Invoice chasing. A billing follow-up agent sends polite reminders on open invoices on the schedule you set and flags the accounts that need a personal call, so collections stop depending on your memory.

Scheduling and reminders. Jobs get booked against your real calendar, with a confirmation, a 24-hour and a 1-hour reminder and a rebook email for no-shows. Past customers who agreed to hear from you can get maintenance reminders, such as an annual water heater flush.

Weekly numbers. A weekly results email built from your real numbers: new leads, bookings, no-shows, reviews and how fast leads got a reply.

Paperwork. A file drop folder in your own Google Drive or SharePoint that AI sorts, so permits, inspection photos and warranty paperwork stop living in a camera roll.

The front office. Every plan includes the standard front office agents too: an AI receptionist for the calls you forward, website chat, review requests after completed jobs, and missed-call text-back once carriers approve your texting registration.

One CEO dashboard instead of a dozen logins

The CEO dashboard shows every connected app live, what every agent is doing, and the numbers that matter to you: open quotes, unpaid invoices, this week’s bookings, new reviews. Common connections include QuickBooks, Stripe, Google, Microsoft 365, Slack and your CRM. If an app has an API, we can connect it.

On the Founding CTO plan and up, you can also ask the dashboard plain-English questions about your live data with “Ask Efficio”, such as “which quotes over $2,000 have no follow-up?” It is read-only, so it answers and never sends anything.
