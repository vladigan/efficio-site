// week one

Six steps, most of them ours.

Everything is built in your own Efficio account, a HighLevel sub-account. Brady installs it; AI does the drafting and building under his review.

Day 0 Day 7 Week 2
1

Sales call Day 0

If it’s a fit, you pick a plan, sign a short order form and pay the $500 setup by card. You get a welcome email and an intake link.

2

Intake Day 0–1 · ~10 min

Your hours, services, the prices the AI may quote, common questions, the number urgent calls go to, your calendar, and your legal business name and EIN (for texting registration).

3

Kickoff Day 1–2 · 30 min

We create your Efficio account and send your login, get the receptionist’s local number, and submit your texting (A2P 10DLC) registration in your business’s name.

4

Build Day 2–4

We write the receptionist’s script from your intake and website, and set up website chat, reminders, no-show and review follow-ups, and your dashboard. You can review the script.

5

Connect and test Day 5

You connect your Google or Outlook calendar and turn on call forwarding (we send instructions for your carrier). We run test calls (after-hours, booking, urgent transfer), a chat, a booking and an opt-out test.

6

Go live Day 6–7 · 20 min

On the go-live call you place your own test call and we walk through your dashboard. The receptionist starts answering real calls, and monthly billing starts that day.

Not live in week one: texting, which waits for carrier approval of your A2P registration; any outbound contact; and, on Founding CTO and Full Custom CTO, the custom agent and custom dashboard, which are built after go-live on a timeline we agree at kickoff. Your first weekly results email arrives in week two.

// the promise

7 business days, or your setup fee back.

If your AI receptionist, booking and live dashboard aren’t live within 7 business days of kickoff, we refund the $500 setup fee. It applies when kickoff is held within 2 business days of payment and you give us call forwarding and calendar access by day 5. Texting (A2P 10DLC approval) is excluded. Refund policy.
