// before you ask

The honest answers.

No pressure. Here's exactly what you're signing up for.

From $497/mo Live in 7 business days Setup back if we miss it Month-to-month
What if it doesn't work for my business?
We qualify honestly on the first call and tell you if we don’t think it will pay for itself. If your receptionist, booking and dashboard aren’t live within 7 business days of kickoff, we refund the $500 setup (kickoff within 2 business days of payment; call forwarding and calendar access from you by day 5; texting approval excluded). After that it’s month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. There’s no results-based refund.
Will my customers know they're talking to AI?
Yes. The receptionist tells every caller it’s an AI assistant and that the call is recorded. It answers from information you approve, books appointments, and transfers anything urgent or sensitive to you.
Do I have to switch tools or learn a new system?
No. You keep your phone number, calendar and website. You forward the calls you miss to the receptionist’s number, connect your Google or Outlook calendar, and we add chat to your site. Your Efficio account (built on HighLevel) has an inbox and a mobile app if you want them, but you don’t have to live in it.
Is “live in 7 business days” actually real?
It’s what we commit to: your receptionist, booking and dashboard live within 7 business days of kickoff, or the setup fee comes back. Texting depends on the carriers approving your business’s A2P registration, which can take longer, so it’s excluded.
How is this different from voicemail or a chatbot?
Voicemail takes a message most callers won’t leave. A chatbot answers questions on one channel. The receptionist answers the phone and books the job, transfers urgent calls, and the rest of the front office sends reminders and review requests, with a dashboard showing all of it.
Whose data is it?
Yours. It lives in your own Efficio account, a HighLevel sub-account, and passes through a short list of providers: HighLevel (LeadConnector), Anthropic, Cloudflare, Stripe, Google Workspace and Brevo. If you leave, you get a CSV export of your contacts and a 30-day wind-down.
