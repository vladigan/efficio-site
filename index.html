// the problem

Aged leads and renewals that nobody works.

Producers call the fresh leads. The rest age in the CRM. Carriers who said “call me at renewal” don't hear back at renewal, even though the data to estimate their renewal date is public.

Aged leads sit untouched

Leads you bought months ago are still in the CRM with one or two attempts on them.

Renewals get missed

Public FMCSA insurance filings give an estimated renewal date for many carriers. Someone has to look it up and follow up in time.

Producers should be quoting

Re-sending emails to no-answers isn't a good use of a producer's hour. Booked conversations with people who replied are.

What the Sprint does inside your GHL.

01

Connect

Limited access to your GoHighLevel through a token you control and can revoke. We build in your account, not ours.

Enrich & rank

Leads with a DOT or MC number are matched to public FMCSA data and ranked by estimated renewal window. Estimates are labeled as estimates.

03

Reactivate

Email you approve, sent from your own domain in your own GHL. First touch to every reachable lead within 48 hours of the Start Date.

Book & review

Replies book onto your producers' calendars. Dashboard live by day 3; on day 14 we review the numbers with you.

When the Sprint clock starts: Day 1 is the Start Date, which is the later of (a) the day your $500 setup fee is paid and (b) the day you complete the access checklist (GoHighLevel access, an authenticated sending domain, your lead and renewal list loaded, producer calendars connected, and a named sponsor). Every Sprint deadline counts from that day.
