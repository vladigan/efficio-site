// the leak

You pay for leads. Most never get a second touch.

Producers call the fresh ones, then the pile ages. Truckers who said "call me at renewal" never hear back at renewal. The data you need to know who renews next month is public, but nobody has time to pull it.

Aged leads sit untouched

Leads you bought months ago are still in the CRM with one or two attempts on them. Many of those carriers are still shopping.

Renewals get missed

A carrier's current insurer and policy effective date show up in public FMCSA filings. That gives you an estimated renewal date, if someone looks it up and follows up in time.

Producers should be quoting

Chasing no-answers and re-sending emails isn't a producer's best hour. Booked conversations with people who replied are.

// how the lead rescue sprint works

Set up and sending in the first week.

The Sprint runs 14 days. Setup and the first reactivation wave happen in week one. Week two is replies, bookings and the numbers review.

01

Connect (days 1–2)

You give us limited access to your GoHighLevel with a token you control and can revoke. We connect your producers' calendars and import your lead and renewal lists. We build in your account, not ours.

02

Enrich & rank (days 1–3)

Each lead and renewal is matched by DOT number to public FMCSA insurance filings: current insurer and effective date, giving an estimated X-date. Then we rank everyone by who renews soonest.

03

Reactivate (days 2–7)

Personal, reviewed email follow-up to every reachable lead within 48 hours of launch. Replies that want to talk book straight onto a producer's calendar.

04

Book & review (days 8–14)

We keep working replies and booking. On day 14 we go through the dashboard with you: who was touched, who replied, who booked, and cost per quoted appointment by lead vendor.

What we connect

  • Your GoHighLevel sub-account, through a least-privilege token you own.
  • Your producers' calendars in GHL, so booked slots land where they already work.
  • Your lead and renewal lists. AgencyZoom, EZLynx and HawkSoft shops can send us a CSV export.
  • Public FMCSA data by DOT number. No scraping behind logins, and no data brokers.

What you get

  • A renewal-window list: every carrier ranked by estimated X-date.
  • Email reactivation that books onto producer calendars, running as workflows in your GHL.
  • A dashboard live by day 3: GHL native, plus one report for lead-vendor ROI.
  • Everything we build stays in your account. Stop after the Sprint and you keep it.

// compliance first

Consent rules are built in, not bolted on.

Texting and calling rules exist for good reasons, and the penalties are per message. Every workflow we build follows these rules from day one.

Consent before contact

Texts go only to people who opted in, from your own registered number (your A2P brand is the sender). Aged and purchased leads get email and your producers' calls, never AI texts or AI calls.

One STOP stops everything

A STOP or unsubscribe on any channel suppresses that contact on every channel: text, email and voice. Not just the one they replied on.

Business hours only

Outbound follow-up goes out between 8 AM and 8 PM in the recipient's local time. No late-night texts from your agency's number.

CAN-SPAM on every email

Every email carries your agency's physical address and a working one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribes are honored right away.

AI says it's AI

Wherever the AI is in the conversation, it identifies itself as an AI assistant for your agency. AI voice handles inbound calls only, unless a contact gave prior express written consent.

Your data stays in your GHL

We work inside your sub-account with a token you can revoke at any time. We don't copy your leads out, resell them or pool them with anyone else's.

The AI never gives coverage advice or binds a policy. It qualifies, books and follows up, and a licensed producer quotes. You own your consent records and your consent policy; this page isn't legal advice.

// questions agencies ask

Straight answers.

Do I need GoHighLevel?

Yes. Everything runs as workflows inside your own GoHighLevel account, so the work and the data stay with you. If you're not on GHL today, tell us on the call and we'll say honestly whether it's worth adding for this.

We run AgencyZoom, EZLynx or HawkSoft. Does that matter?

Not for the Sprint. You export your leads and renewals as a CSV and we work them in GHL. Bind data lives in your agency management system, so on AI Operator we report binds from a monthly AMS export you upload. A direct AMS integration is a separate, one-time quote, and only once a real API path is confirmed.

Will you text or call our aged leads with AI?

No. Purchased and aged leads usually don't carry the consent needed for texts or AI calls, so the Sprint uses email plus your producers' own calls. Texts go only to contacts who opted in. AI voice is inbound-only unless a contact gave prior express written consent.

What counts as one of the 5 appointments?

An appointment counts toward the 5 (a “qualifying appointment”) when all three are true: (1) it's booked through the Sprint's email outreach onto one of your producers' calendars during the 14-day Sprint; (2) it's with a unique contact from your own list — duplicates, test records, your staff, and anyone already in an active quote with you in the 14 days before the Sprint started don't count; and (3) your producer takes it. Your producers take each booked appointment within 1 business day of it being booked: they confirm it, call the prospect at the booked time, or reschedule it with the prospect. An appointment your team doesn't take within that 1 business day still counts, because the booking was delivered and the miss was on your side. Once 5 qualifying appointments happen, the $500 performance portion is due.

What if you book fewer than 5?

Then the $500 performance portion isn't charged (or is refunded if you prepaid it). The $500 setup is non-refundable because it pays for the build: the enrichment, the workflows and the dashboard, which stay in your account either way.

How is AI Operator billed?

$1,500 a month by card on file, with a 90-day minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice. Messaging and call usage is passed through at cost. If you start within 7 days of your Sprint ending, 50% of the Sprint fee you paid ($500 on the full $1,000) is credited to your first month. The Sprint credit and any service credit never stack.

Does the AI give coverage advice or bind policies?

Never. It qualifies, books and follows up. Quoting and advice stay with your licensed producers, and we keep a review log of every prompt and script we put in front of your leads.
