Welcome aboard. Three things to start.

Work starts once these are done. Nothing is sent to your leads until you've approved the templates.

  1. 1

    Book your kickoff call

    30 minutes with Brady. We confirm scope and walk through the checklist below. Book kickoff →

  2. 2

    Complete the access checklist

    • An active GoHighLevel account in your agency's name (you pay for it directly).
    • A least-privilege GHL user or private-integration token for Efficio, which you can revoke at any time.
    • A sending domain or sender identity in your agency's name, authenticated for email (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), or permission for us to set that up.
    • Your lead and renewal list loaded into GHL (or an AMS export for you to import), with lead source, date acquired and DOT or MC number where known.
    • Approximate lead cost by vendor, for the cost-per-appointment view.
    • Producer calendars connected in GHL, with real availability, time zone and business hours.
    • A named sponsor (owner or sales manager) who can direct producers, plus your existing do-not-contact and unsubscribe lists.
  3. 3

    Fill in the kickoff form

    Ten questions, about 7 minutes. Open the kickoff form →

When the Sprint clock starts: Day 1 is the Start Date, which is the later of (a) the day your $500 setup fee is paid and (b) the day you complete the access checklist (GoHighLevel access, an authenticated sending domain, your lead and renewal list loaded, producer calendars connected, and a named sponsor). Every Sprint deadline counts from that day.

AI Operator day-7 promise: the first automation that needs no carrier or A2P approval (email speed-to-lead, the dashboard or routing) is live by day 7 after you complete the access checklist, or month 1 is free. Carrier and A2P 10DLC approvals are outside our control and are excluded.
