// before you commit

Pricing questions, answered.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

The Lead Rescue Sprint has a $500 setup fee, due at signing and non-refundable once the Sprint starts; it pays for the build, which stays in your account. The other $500 is due only after 5 qualifying appointments. AI Operator is $1,500/mo with a 90-day minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice, billed by card on file.

When does the Sprint clock start?

Day 1 is the Start Date: the later of the day your $500 setup fee is paid and the day you complete the access checklist (what's on it). The 48-hour first touch, the day-3 deliverables and the 14-day window all count from that day.

What if the Sprint doesn't book 5 appointments?

Then the $500 performance portion isn't charged (or is refunded if you prepaid it). The $500 setup is non-refundable; you keep the enriched lists, the workflows and the dashboard we built in your GoHighLevel.

What counts as one of the 5 appointments?

An appointment counts toward the 5 (a “qualifying appointment”) when all three are true: (1) it's booked through the Sprint's email outreach onto one of your producers' calendars during the 14-day Sprint; (2) it's with a unique contact from your own list — duplicates, test records, your staff, and anyone already in an active quote with you in the 14 days before the Sprint started don't count; and (3) your producer takes it. Your producers take each booked appointment within 1 business day of it being booked: they confirm it, call the prospect at the booked time, or reschedule it with the prospect. An appointment your team doesn't take within that 1 business day still counts, because the booking was delivered and the miss was on your side.

What's the day-7 promise on AI Operator?

If the first automation that needs no carrier or A2P approval (email speed-to-lead, dashboard or routing) isn't live by day 7 after you complete the access checklist, month 1's fee is waived; anything that depends on carrier, A2P 10DLC or other third-party approval is excluded. The access checklist is the logins, permissions and details we need to start. Credits never stack.

Do I get credit for the Sprint if I keep going?

Yes. If you sign AI Operator within 7 days of the Sprint ending, 50% of the Sprint fee you paid ($500 on the full $1,000) is credited to your first month. The Sprint credit and any service credit never stack.

What about Founding CTO and Full Custom CTO?

Both are custom quote. We scope them on a call once we know what you need built, so the price matches the work.
