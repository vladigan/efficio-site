// the terms, in plain English

What you're agreeing to.

Setup, billing and cancelling

  • Setup ($500) is paid on the sales call, by card.
  • Monthly billing starts on go-live day, the day your receptionist starts answering real calls.
  • Month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days' notice. No partial-month refunds.
  • Your data is yours. If you leave, you get a CSV export of your contacts and a 30-day wind-down.

The 7-business-day promise

  • If your AI receptionist, booking and live dashboard aren't live within 7 business days of kickoff, we refund the $500 setup fee.
  • It applies when kickoff is held within 2 business days of payment and you give us call forwarding and calendar access by day 5.
  • Texting (A2P 10DLC registration and missed-call text-back) depends on carrier approval and is not covered.
  • That's the only guarantee. Full wording in our refund policy and terms.

Founding clients

  • We're signing our first clients now. For our first three, the monthly price is locked for 12 months in exchange for a 20-minute results review at day 30 and, only with your written approval, permission to publish your real numbers.

// before you book

Pricing questions, answered.

Which plan is right for me?

We decide it with you before or on the call. If you get at least 50 calls or leads a month (or spend $2,000+ a month on ads or leads) and your average job is $1,000 or more, we’ll suggest Managed. Otherwise, Core. If your average job is under $500, we'll tell you honestly that this probably isn't worth it for you yet.

Is there a contract?

You sign a short order form on the call. Both plans are month-to-month after go-live, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. There are no partial-month refunds.

What does the $500 setup cover, and when is it refunded?

It covers building your account: the receptionist's script and FAQs, your calendar and reminders, website chat, the consent and opt-out setup, and your dashboard. If your receptionist, booking and dashboard aren't live within 7 business days of kickoff, we refund it, as long as kickoff happened within 2 business days of payment and you gave us call forwarding and calendar access by day 5. Texting approval (A2P 10DLC) isn't covered because the carriers control it.

Why do I need a new phone number?

You keep your main number. The receptionist gets its own local number, and you forward calls you don't answer (and after-hours calls) to it. We send you the forwarding steps for your carrier.

What is HighLevel, and why do you say you're built on it?

HighLevel (also called LeadConnector) is the CRM and messaging platform your Efficio account runs on. We set it up, run it and brand it as Efficio, but we don't claim it as our own software. Your contacts and conversations stay in your account.

Can the AI call or text my leads for me?

Not cold. The receptionist answers inbound calls only. Texts go only to people who gave consent, and every opt-out is honored on every channel. We don't do outbound robocalls, cold texting or purchased-list dialing.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. We take the card on the call once we both agree it's a fit, so nobody pays for a plan that doesn't suit their business.
