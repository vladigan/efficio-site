// before you commit

Pricing questions, answered.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

The Lead Rescue Sprint has a $500 non-refundable setup fee, due at kickoff; it pays for the build, which stays in your account. The other $500 is due only after 5 AI-booked appointments your team takes. AI Operator is $1,500/mo with a 90-day minimum, billed by card on file.

What if the Sprint doesn't book 5 appointments?

Then the $500 performance portion isn't due. You keep the enriched lists, the workflows and the dashboard we built in your GoHighLevel.

What's the day-7 promise on AI Operator?

Your first automation is live by day 7 or month 1 is free. It covers automations that need no carrier approval, like email speed-to-lead, the dashboard and routing. Carrier or A2P 10DLC texting approvals are outside our control and excluded. Credits never stack.

Do I get credit for the Sprint if I keep going?

Yes. If you sign AI Operator within 7 days of the Sprint ending, 50% of the Sprint fee ($500) is credited to your first month.

What about Founding CTO and Full Custom CTO?

Both are custom quote. We scope them on a call once we know what you need built, so the price matches the work.
