// before you book

Pricing questions, answered.

Which plan is right for me?

We decide it with you on the call. AI Operator is the AI front office itself: receptionist, chat, booking, reviews and a live dashboard, tuned every month. It fits best when you get at least 50 calls or leads a month (or spend $2,000+ a month on ads or leads) and your average job is $1,000 or more. Founding CTO adds a custom AI agent and a custom dashboard over your live data, for when the standard front office isn't enough. Full Custom CTO adds ongoing custom engineering, a weekly call and a 24-hour response time. If your average job is under $500, we'll tell you honestly that this probably isn't worth it for you yet.

Is there a contract?

You sign a short order form on the call. All three plans are billed monthly from go-live day and are month-to-month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. There are no partial-month refunds.

What does the $500 setup cover, and when is it refunded?

It's the same $500 on every plan. It covers building your account: the receptionist's script and FAQs, your calendar and reminders, website chat, the consent and opt-out setup, and your dashboard. If your receptionist, booking and dashboard aren't live within 7 business days of kickoff, we refund it, as long as kickoff happened within 2 business days of payment and you gave us call forwarding and calendar access by day 5. Texting approval (A2P 10DLC) isn't covered because the carriers control it.

Why do I need a new phone number?

You keep your main number. The receptionist gets its own local number, and you forward calls you don't answer (and after-hours calls) to it. We send you the forwarding steps for your carrier.

What is HighLevel, and why do you say you're built on it?

HighLevel (also called LeadConnector) is the CRM and messaging platform your Efficio account runs on. We set it up, run it and brand it as Efficio, but we don't claim it as our own software. Your contacts and conversations stay in your account.

Can the AI call or text my leads for me?

Not cold. The receptionist answers inbound calls only. Texts go only to people who gave consent, and every opt-out is honored on every channel. We don't do outbound robocalls, cold texting or purchased-list dialing.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. We take the card on the call once we both agree it's a fit, so nobody pays for a plan that doesn't suit their business.
