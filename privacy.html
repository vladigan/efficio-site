Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

BNG Contracting Enterprise LLC, doing business as Efficio ("we," "us," or "our"), operates the website efficio.tech and the Efficio service. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information in connection with the Efficio AI front office service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different categories of information depending on your relationship with us:

From our clients (businesses we work for):

  • Contact and business details: name, email address, business name, phone number, and the details you give us to set up your AI front office, such as hours, services, prices the AI may quote, answers to common questions, the number urgent calls transfer to, your website, and your legal business name and EIN (needed for texting registration).
  • Payment information: billing data processed by Stripe. We never store your full card number.
  • Engagement records: order forms, approved scripts and the reports we prepare for you.

Data in your Efficio account (Client Data):

  • Information about your callers, leads and customers, such as contact details, call recordings and transcripts, chat and text conversations, and appointments, is stored in your own Efficio account, a HighLevel (LeadConnector) sub-account. It belongs to you.
  • We access it only to set up, run and support the service for you. We don't sell it, we don't pool it with any other client's data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Website visitors:

  • Pages visited, session duration, browser type, device information, and IP address, collected through analytics and advertising tags, only if you accept them in our cookie banner (see Section 6).
  • Information you submit through the questionnaire, onboarding, text opt-in, chat or call-booking forms on efficio.tech.

We use prospect information to answer your inquiry, prepare for a call you booked, and, if you become a client, to set up the work. We do not sell or rent prospect information, and we do not share it with our clients.

2. How We Use Information

  • To set up and run the service in your Efficio account
  • To answer your questions and prepare for calls you book
  • To process payments and manage billing
  • To send transactional messages (scheduling, onboarding, billing and your weekly results email)
  • To provide support

3. Email, CAN-SPAM, and Messages Sent From Client Accounts

Email from Efficio. Efficio does not send cold email from efficio.tech. Email we send about our own services includes a way to opt out, and we honor opt-outs promptly: our standard is the same day, and never later than the 10 business days the law allows. To stop email from Efficio, use the opt-out in the email, visit efficio.tech/unsubscribe, or email contact@efficio.tech.

Messages from a business that uses Efficio. Email, texts and calls sent from a client's Efficio account are sent in that client's name, and the client is the sender of record. In the accounts we set up, an opt-out on any channel is applied as do-not-disturb on every channel, automated texts go out only between 8 AM and 8 PM in the contact's time zone, and texts and AI calls go only to contacts with the consent the law requires. If you want a business that uses Efficio to stop contacting you, reply STOP, use the unsubscribe link, contact that business, or email us and we'll pass the request on.

4. Text Messages and Calls from Efficio's AI Assistant

Our opt-in page, the follow-up form on Find your plan, the pre-call questionnaire and the client onboarding form each show two separate boxes next to the phone number. Both are optional and start unchecked, and neither is a condition of buying anything:

  • Texts. If you tick the text box, Efficio (BNG Contracting Enterprise LLC) may send recurring marketing and account text messages to the mobile number you gave, including messages sent using automated technology: for example booking confirmations, appointment reminders, replies to your questions and information about our services.
  • Calls, including from Efficio's AI assistant. If you tick the call box, Efficio (BNG Contracting Enterprise LLC) may call that number, including marketing calls and voicemails, using automated technology (including an automated system to select and dial numbers), prerecorded messages, and an artificial or AI-generated voice. Calls may be recorded.

For each box you tick, we record the date and time, the page, the phone number, the exact wording you agreed to and its version, and the network address the form was sent from. If you tick neither box, we won't send you marketing texts or place automated or AI calls to you; we may still reply to a text or call you start.

  • Consent is voluntary. You are never required to agree to receive text messages or calls as a condition of purchasing any product or service.
  • Message frequency varies, up to 4 text messages a week.
  • Msg & data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • STOP and HELP. Reply STOP to any message to stop. After STOP we send one confirmation that you've been unsubscribed and no further texts to that number. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at contact@efficio.tech or (786) 383-1160.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw either consent at any time in any reasonable way, for example by replying STOP, saying "stop calling" on a call, emailing contact@efficio.tech or brady@efficio.tech, or calling 716-303-4265. We honor it promptly.
  • Where your number and consent are kept. Your name, email, mobile number and consent record are stored in the form log on our website host, Cloudflare, which keeps proof of your consent, and in Efficio's CRM, GoHighLevel, which we use to send texts and place calls. Your number is shared only with the providers that deliver our texts and calls (our CRM's texting and calling service and the phone carriers that carry them), and only to deliver them.
  • Used only for these messages and calls. Your number and consent are used only for the texts and calls described next to the boxes you ticked.
  • Never sent to advertising platforms. Your number and consent are never sent to Meta, Google, LinkedIn or any other advertising platform, never sold, and never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. The opt-in page loads no analytics or advertising tags.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties other than the service providers named above that store it and deliver the messages. To exercise any of your rights, see "Your Rights, Opt-Out, and Deletion" below.

5. Subprocessors

Your data does not stay on a single system of ours. To run the service, it passes through a short list of third-party providers ("subprocessors"). Each receives only the data it needs for its function, under that provider's own privacy and security commitments. The current list is:

  • HighLevel (LeadConnector) (the platform your Efficio account runs on: CRM, calendar, phone numbers, AI voice receptionist, website chat, text messaging, email and dashboards) — stores client account data, including callers' and customers' contact details, call recordings and conversations, and Efficio's own prospect and client records. See HighLevel's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes prompts and content for AI features Efficio builds outside HighLevel, such as drafting your weekly results email and answering “Ask Efficio” questions. Inputs are processed to return a response; we do not opt in to training. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (DNS, CDN, edge worker, and bot/abuse protection) — processes IP address, request headers, and request payloads in order to serve efficio.tech and receive our web forms, including the form log that keeps proof of text and call consent. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe (payments and subscription billing) — processes payment method, name, billing address, and email when you pay a setup fee or subscription. We do not store full card numbers. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Workspace (Efficio's email and calendar) — processes the email you exchange with us and the calendar details of calls you book with us. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Brevo (Efficio's own transactional email) — processes recipient email address, sender details, and message content to send email from Efficio, such as a welcome email after you sign up. See Brevo's Privacy Policy.

Our website also uses GitHub (static site hosting, via GitHub Pages; see GitHub's Privacy Statement) and, only if you accept cookies, the analytics and advertising tags below:

  • Google (Google Analytics 4) (website analytics) — loads only if you accept analytics and advertising cookies. It collects pages visited, device and browser information, and IP-derived data such as approximate location. This data is not anonymous. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms (Meta Pixel) (advertising measurement) — loads only if you accept. It records page views, booked calls and client onboarding-form submissions on efficio.tech (no form contents) and sets cookies that Meta can link to a Facebook or Instagram account. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn (LinkedIn Insight Tag) (advertising measurement) — loads only if you accept. It records page views and conversions and sets cookies LinkedIn can link to a LinkedIn account. See LinkedIn's Privacy Policy.

This list can change as we add or replace tools. Material additions will be reflected in this section, and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Analytics and advertising tags on efficio.tech are off until you accept them in our cookie banner. Until you click Accept, none of the tags below is loaded, no request is made to Google, Meta or LinkedIn for them, and none of their cookies is set.

What loads after you click Accept:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google): measures visits and site usage, including pages viewed, device and browser information, and IP-derived data such as approximate location. This is not anonymous data.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta): used to measure whether our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads lead to visits and booked calls. It records page views, booked calls and client onboarding-form submissions (without the form contents).
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn): used to measure whether our LinkedIn ads lead to visits and conversions.

These tags set cookies and collect device, browsing and IP-derived information, which Google, Meta and LinkedIn process under their own privacy policies (linked in Section 5). Conversion events, such as a booked call, are sent to them only if you have accepted.

If you decline, or don't choose, none of these tags loads. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. You can change it at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page; if you switch to Decline, we remove the tags' cookies from efficio.tech and reload the page so they stop running.

Booking calendar: our booking calendar is hosted by GoHighLevel and loads Meta’s tracking script inside it, so we treat it the same way: it only loads after you accept. If you decline, you can book by emailing brady@efficio.tech.

Chat widget: our chat is run by GoHighLevel. Its scripts store visitor and attribution identifiers in your browser, so it is also gated: until you accept, only a “Chat (requires cookies)” button is shown and nothing is requested from GoHighLevel. If you later switch to Decline, we remove the chat’s stored identifiers from efficio.tech and reload the page.

Exception, direct links to the booking page: some links open the booking page on GoHighLevel’s own site instead of inside ours (for example “Open it in a new tab” on our booking page, and many “Book a call” links across the site). Following one of those links leaves efficio.tech, and that GoHighLevel-hosted page loads its own scripts and trackers, including Meta’s tracking script, whatever you chose in our banner. Their use is governed by HighLevel’s privacy policy (linked in Section 5). To avoid them, email brady@efficio.tech and we’ll book the call for you.

You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Client accounts live on HighLevel's infrastructure. We access them through named users or read-only tokens that the client can revoke, and we keep client credentials in a password manager, not in code. If we confirm unauthorized access to client data in our control, we notify the client promptly.

8. Data Retention

  • Client Data: stays in your Efficio account while you're a client. When service ends, you receive a CSV export of your contacts and a 30-day wind-down, after which the account is closed.
  • Prospect and client contact records: kept for as long as needed to respond to you and manage the relationship, then deleted on request.
  • Consent records and suppression lists: kept so we can prove consent and keep honoring opt-outs.
  • Billing records: kept as required by tax and accounting law.

For people who contacted a business that uses Efficio: that business controls the information in its account. Contact the business directly, or email us and we'll pass your request on and help the business act on it.

How to opt out or request deletion:

  • To stop email from Efficio: use the opt-out in any email we send you, or visit efficio.tech/unsubscribe.
  • To stop text messages: reply STOP to any text (reply HELP for help). See Section 4 for details.
  • To request deletion of your data or exercise any other right above: email contact@efficio.tech from the address on file (or include enough information for us to identify your record). We confirm and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Records kept because of a legal obligation (for example, billing records or suppression entries) are described in Section 8.
