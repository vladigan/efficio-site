Refund Policy & Guarantee

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This page sets out when fees are refundable and when they aren't. It covers both plans: Front Office Core ($497 a month) and Front Office Managed ($1,500 a month), each with a one-time $500 setup fee. There is no results-based or savings-based refund on either plan.

1. The 7-business-day go-live promise

If your AI receptionist, booking and live dashboard aren't live within 7 business days of your kickoff call, we refund your $500 setup fee in full.

  • Conditions: the promise applies when (a) the kickoff call is held within 2 business days of your payment, and (b) you give us call forwarding and calendar access by business day 5 after kickoff.
  • What “live” means: the receptionist answers calls forwarded to its number and books them into your calendar, and your dashboard shows those calls and bookings.
  • Not covered: text messaging. Missed-call text-back and other texts can only start after the carriers approve your business's A2P 10DLC registration, which we don't control, so their timing is excluded.
  • This is the only guarantee we offer.

2. Monthly billing and cancelling

  • The $500 setup fee is paid on the sales call. Monthly billing starts on go-live day, on the card you gave us or through the monthly payment link.
  • Both plans are month-to-month. Either side may cancel with 30 days' written notice; service and billing continue through the notice period.
  • We do not refund partial months or unused portions of a billing period.
  • Voice minutes over your plan's allowance ($0.25 a minute), carrier registration fees and text usage are billed as described on the pricing page and aren't refundable once used.
  • When you cancel, you get a CSV export of your contacts and a 30-day wind-down.

3. If we can't serve your business

After payment we ask for a few intake details, including your U.S. business website and EIN (the carriers require the EIN for texting registration). If those show we can't serve your business, we refund the setup fee and cancel.

4. How to request a refund

Email contact@efficio.tech from the address on your account and include your kickoff date. We send approved refunds to your original payment method within 10 business days.

5. Where this fits

This policy is the refund policy referenced in our Terms of Service (Sections 1 and 3). For the plans, see pricing. Questions before you start? Book a call.
