Refund Policy & Guarantee

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This page sets out when fees are refundable and when they aren't. There are two published offers: the Lead Rescue Sprint and AI Operator. There is no measurable-savings refund on any plan.

1. The 7-day go-live promise (AI Operator)

Your first AI Operator automation is live within seven days after you complete the access checklist (the logins, permissions and details we need to start). If we miss day seven for reasons within our control, month 1 is free: it is applied as a service credit, not a cash refund. Carrier approvals and A2P 10DLC texting registration are outside our control and are excluded from every day-7 promise.

2. Lead Rescue Sprint ($1,000)

  • Setup fee: $500, non-refundable once the Sprint starts. It pays for the build (enrichment, workflows and dashboard), which stays in your account.
  • Performance portion: $500, earned only when 5 qualifying appointments happen within the 14-day Sprint Window. If fewer than 5 happen, the performance portion is never charged; if you prepaid it, it is refunded to your original payment method within 10 business days of the day-14 review.
  • What counts as a qualifying appointment: An appointment counts toward the 5 (a “qualifying appointment”) when all three are true: (1) it’s booked through the Sprint’s email outreach onto one of your producers’ calendars during the 14-day Sprint; (2) it’s with a unique contact from your own list — duplicates, test records, your staff, and anyone already in an active quote with you in the 14 days before the Sprint started don’t count; and (3) your producer takes it. Your producers take each booked appointment within 1 business day of it being booked: they confirm it, call the prospect at the booked time, or reschedule it with the prospect. An appointment your team doesn’t take within that 1 business day still counts, because the booking was delivered and the miss was on your side.
  • AI Operator credit: if you sign AI Operator within 7 calendar days after the Sprint ends, 50% of the Sprint fee you paid ($500 on the full $1,000) is credited to AI Operator month 1. The credit is used once, isn't transferable, has no cash value, and expires if unused.
  • Credits never stack. The Sprint credit and any service credit (including the day-7 “month 1 free” credit) never combine. If more than one could apply, only one applies, once: the one worth more to you.

3. AI Operator ($1,500/month)

  • Billed monthly by card on file, with a 90-day minimum term.
  • After the 90-day minimum, AI Operator continues month-to-month. Either side may cancel with 30 days' written notice; service and billing continue through the notice period.
  • We do not refund partial months or unused portions of a billing period, and there is no measurable-savings refund.
  • Messaging, call and platform usage is passed through at cost.
  • Founding CTO, Full Custom CTO and other custom arrangements are quoted, and their terms are set in a written agreement.

4. How refunds are paid

You don't need to claim a refund. If you prepaid the Sprint's $500 performance portion and the 5-appointment threshold isn't met, we refund it automatically to your original payment method within 10 business days of the day-14 review, as set out in your statement of work (§5). Questions about a refund? Email contact@efficio.tech.
