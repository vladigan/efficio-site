Show the math no hidden assumptions

The estimate uses only your three numbers and one conversion from weeks to months:

1Missed calls a month = your missed calls per week × 4.33 (52 weeks ÷ 12 months).
2Would have booked = missed calls a month × the share you said would have booked.
3Missed-call revenue per month = jobs that would have booked × your average job value. The yearly figure is × 12.

It uses job value, not profit, and it doesn’t account for callers who call back later. Treat it as a rough ballpark to sanity-check, not a forecast.
