// what we do

Three things, one account.

every plan/

The front office

An inbound AI voice receptionist, website chat, booking confirmations and reminders, a no-show rebook email, review requests, and missed-call text-back once your texting registration is approved.

every plan/

The dashboard and reporting

A live dashboard of calls answered, after-hours calls caught, bookings, no-shows, reviews and speed-to-lead, plus a weekly results email and a monthly review call.

tuning/

Tuning and custom builds

Every plan: two hours a month of call review and tuning by Brady and one new automation a month from a set menu. Founding CTO adds a custom AI agent and a custom dashboard over your live data with “Ask Efficio”; Full Custom CTO adds ongoing custom engineering.

The full list for each plan is on What’s included.

// by bottleneck

Where it pays off first.

after-hours/

Calls after you close

Forward after-hours calls to the receptionist. It answers, books into your calendar and transfers anything urgent to your cell.

on the job/

Calls you can’t pick up

When you’re with a customer, calls you don’t answer roll over to the receptionist instead of voicemail.

no-shows/

Empty slots

Confirmations, 24-hour and 1-hour reminders and a no-show rebook email keep booked appointments on the calendar.

reviews/

Reviews you never asked for

A review request goes out after every completed appointment.

follow-up/

Leads that go quiet

An instant reply to web-form leads or follow-up on open estimates, added as your monthly automations.

visibility/

Not knowing what came in

The dashboard and weekly email show every call, booking and review, so you know what the front office did.
