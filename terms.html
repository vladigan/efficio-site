1. Description of Services

Efficio installs and runs an AI front office for small businesses. It is built on HighLevel (also known as LeadConnector): each client gets its own Efficio-branded HighLevel sub-account at app.efficio.tech. Depending on the plan, the service includes:

  • An AI voice receptionist that answers inbound calls forwarded to its number, tells callers it is an AI and that the call is recorded, answers from the client's approved information, books appointments into the client's calendar, takes messages and transfers urgent calls
  • AI website chat
  • Booking confirmations, reminders and no-show follow-up
  • Missed-call text-back and review requests, sent only to contacts with the required consent and only after the client's A2P 10DLC texting registration is approved
  • A live dashboard and a weekly results email
  • On every plan (AI Operator and above): 1,000 voice minutes a month, up to 2 hours a month of hands-on optimization, one new automation a month from a set menu, and a monthly review call
  • On Founding CTO: a custom AI agent built for the client's business, a custom read-only dashboard over the client's data with read-only “Ask Efficio” questions, and a call every two weeks
  • On Full Custom CTO: everything in Founding CTO, plus ongoing custom engineering (new automations, agents and integrations), a weekly call and a 24-hour response time

The AI receptionist handles inbound calls only. Efficio does not place cold calls, send cold texts or cold email for clients, dial purchased lists, or handle patient health information. What each plan includes is listed on our pricing page.
